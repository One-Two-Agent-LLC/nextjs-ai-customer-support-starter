# Northstar Studio demo

Northstar Studio is a fictional project workspace used to demonstrate OneTwoAgent integration. All account values are synthetic and isolated per visitor. No real billing, emails or external jobs are triggered.

Projects show a title, status, description and open tasks. "At risk" means the project needs attention; the description explains the current blocker. The Sandbox workspace begins empty.

Record demo activity consumes ten synthetic credits in the active workspace, up to its quota. To get the current remaining balance, use the authorized usage lookup. Do not infer another workspace's balance.

The app supplies recorded plan context when the customer signs in. Live usage and the approved projects view are read through its backend. A handoff sends the conversation to the workspace's team; do not promise immediate availability. Team members take over from the OneTwoAgent Inbox.
